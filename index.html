Your Pathway to ICT Confidence and Compliance

Addressing the urgent need for digitally competent teachers under Zambia's Enhanced Competency-Based Curriculum (ECBC)

The Challenge for Zambian Teachers

The integration of ICT as a compulsory subject in Zambian schools has created an urgent need for:

  • Digitally competent teachers across all subject areas
  • Practical exposure to emerging ICT fields
  • Continuous professional development outside traditional colleges
  • Affordable, structured, and supportive learning environments

Despite policy progress, many teachers face constraints such as:

  • Limited access to modern computer laboratories
  • Inadequate opportunities for advanced ICT training
  • Lack of guided self-learning environments
  • High personal costs for independent online learning

How FutureSkills Addresses These Gaps

FutureSkills offers a district-level, teacher-centered ICT learning ecosystem that blends hosted self-paced online study with structured local support.

About FutureSkills ICT & Digital Skills Centre

FutureSkills ICT & Digital Skills Centre is a Zambian startup dedicated to developing digital skills, strengthening workforce capabilities, and promoting innovation through technology. Originally established as ClickIT Academy and registered in 2019, the Centre's planned launch was delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic. Today, FutureSkills is being revitalized and expanded in response to Zambia's Enhanced Competency-Based Curriculum and the growing demand for industry-relevant digital skills.

Our mission is to empower learners, educators, professionals, entrepreneurs, and organisations with future-ready competencies through accessible, practical, and guided ICT learning. We are building a collaborative hub for digital skills development, teacher capacity building, innovation, technology transfer, and entrepreneurship.

As we enter this launch phase, we are actively seeking strategic partnerships with universities, industry, development organisations, and technology leaders who share our vision of expanding access to high-quality digital education. Through these collaborations, we aim to accelerate knowledge exchange, strengthen local capacity, and create sustainable opportunities that prepare communities for the digital economy.

Welcome to FutureSkills Chingola

We are an emerging ICT skills training center based in Chingola, Zambia, dedicated to bridging the digital skills gap in our community and beyond. Located in the heart of the Copperbelt Province, our center serves as a hub for digital innovation and teacher empowerment.

Our mission is to provide affordable, practical, and guided ICT learning environments that enable teachers and professionals to acquire globally relevant digital skills aligned with national education priorities. We focus on solving the critical shortage of digitally competent teachers in Zambian schools.
